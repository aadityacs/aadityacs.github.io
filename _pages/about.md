I'm a postdoc at Northwestern University, working at the intersection of design optimization, computational mechanics, and machine learning under the supervision of [Dr. Wei Chen](https://ideal.mech.northwestern.edu/).

I have a PhD in mechanical engineering from UW Madison. Advised by [Krishnan Suresh](https://directory.engr.wisc.edu/me/faculty/suresh_krishnan), my research primarily focused on physics constrained machine learning, primarily for design optimization. Along these lines, I've worked on algorithms for optimizing material selection during design, design representation using neural networks and imposing manufacturing constraints in design optimization.

I was previously a postdoc at Argonne National Laboratory working on physics informed X-ray diffraction image analysis. I was also a PhD resident at Google X, working on an undisclosed project related to inverse design of photonic devices.
